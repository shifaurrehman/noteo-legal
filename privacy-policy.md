# Noteo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-17_

This policy explains what data Noteo handles and why. The short version: your notes and voice recordings stay on your device unless you explicitly turn on cloud sync, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address. The notes, categories, and settings you create are content you control — it exists to make the app work for you. We do not collect any other personal information.

## 2. How Your Data Is Used

- **Without an account (free, local mode):** everything stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **With cloud sync (Noteo Pro):** if you subscribe to Noteo Pro and sign in, your notes, categories, and settings sync over an encrypted connection to our secure backend so they are available across your devices.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.

## 3. On-Device Voice Transcription

Voice-to-text transcription runs entirely on your device. The speech model is downloaded once from huggingface.co and works offline afterwards. Your audio recordings are never uploaded and never leave your device — on any plan, including Pro.

## 4. Noteo Pro Subscriptions and Payments

Noteo Pro is billed as an in-app subscription through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat as our subscription-management service. Payment information is handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never see or store your card details. We receive only the subscription status needed to unlock Pro features (for example: active, expired, renewal date).

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Sign-in tokens are stored in your device's secure keychain, and all communication with our backend is encrypted in transit. Cloud-synced data (Pro) is stored on our servers and is only accessible to you through your authenticated account.

## 6. Crash Reporting

The app includes crash reporting through Sentry to help us fix failures. Reports contain no personal information: identifiers are stripped, breadcrumbs are discarded, and default personal data collection is disabled. Aside from crash reporting and the subscription service described above, we do not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual notes and categories at any time from within the app. If you cancel Noteo Pro, your data stops syncing and is retained until you delete it or your account. Deleting your account from Settings permanently removes your account and synced data in about 30 days, and you can restore it by logging in before then.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and on this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
